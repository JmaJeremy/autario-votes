**Andrea Khanjin** is the Member of Provincial Parliament for **Barrie—Innisfil**. She was first elected in 2018, re-elected in 2022, and won her third term with 54.11% of the vote in the February 2025 general election.

She serves as Ontario's **Minister of Red Tape Reduction** in Premier Doug Ford's government.

### Cabinet Roles & Experience
* **Minister of Red Tape Reduction** (Current)
* **Minister of the Environment, Conservation and Parks** (2023–2025)
* **Deputy Government House Leader** (2021–2023)
* **Parliamentary Assistant to the Minister of the Environment, Conservation and Parks** (2019–2023)

### Background & Community
Andrea's family fled the Soviet Union amid its collapse and antisemitic persecution, first to Cuba, then to Canada. Growing up in Barrie, she attended local schools and earned a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from the University of Ottawa.

Before her election as MPP, Khanjin served for over a decade in various policy, communications, and advisory roles within the federal government, including working alongside key cabinet ministers.

### Local Priorities
* Protecting Lake Simcoe water quality, shoreline ecology, and watershed protection.
* Advocating for the revitalization and expansion of the Royal Victoria Regional Health Centre (RVH) South Campus in Innisfil.
* Supporting GO Transit rail enhancements along the Barrie Line and the Bradford Bypass connection.
