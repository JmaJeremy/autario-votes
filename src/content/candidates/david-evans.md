**David Evans** is the **Mayor of the Township of Tiny**, elected in October 2022 to lead the municipality through a period of critical infrastructure and environmental review.

### Priorities & Representation
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Represents Tiny Township on the 32-member County Council.
* **Water Quality & Alliston Aquifer Protection**: Staunch protector of Tiny's pristine groundwater aquifers, known internationally as some of the purest natural water on Earth.
* **Shoreline & Coastal Management**: Managing 70 kilometres of Georgian Bay beachfront, public access points, and seasonal visitor logistics.
