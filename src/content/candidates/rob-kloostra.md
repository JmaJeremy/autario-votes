**Rob Kloostra** is a certified candidate for Councillor in Ward 2 of the City of Orillia in the 2026 municipal election.

Kloostra previously served two terms on Orillia City Council representing Ward 1 from 2014 to 2022. During his council terms, Kloostra advocated strongly for recreation infrastructure, trail maintenance, local sports facilities, and small business support.

In the 2026 municipal election, Kloostra is contesting one of the two councillor positions for Ward 2.
