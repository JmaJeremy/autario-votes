**Mike Burkett** is the **Mayor of the Township of Severn**, serving continuously as Mayor since 2010 and re-elected for a fourth term in October 2022.

### Experience & Focus
* **County of Simcoe Council**: One of the longest-tenured active mayors on County Council, serving on performance audit and governance committees.
* **Trent-Severn Waterway & Tourism**: Preserving the recreational waterways and supporting sustainable eco-tourism throughout Coldwater, Washago, and Severn Bridge.
* **Rural Road Safety & Upgrades**: Maintaining Severn's expansive rural bridge and road network across hundreds of kilometres of mixed terrain.
