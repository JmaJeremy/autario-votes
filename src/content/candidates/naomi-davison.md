**Naomi Davison** was the Ontario Liberal Party candidate in the **York—Simcoe** provincial by-election on September 3, 2026, earning 8,942 votes (38.76%) and delivering a competitive contest across the riding.

Davison is an experienced community leader and advocate. Her by-election campaign focused on reducing wait times at Southlake Regional Health Centre, environmental stewardship of the Lake Simcoe and Holland River watersheds, public transit affordability, and sustainable regional development in Bradford West Gwillimbury.
