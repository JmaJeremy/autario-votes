**Lisa-Marie Wilson** is an elected Trustee for the **Simcoe County District School Board (SCDSB)**, representing the public school electorate across **Barrie Wards 7, 8, 9, and 10** (South Barrie).

### Education Focus & Community Engagement
* **South Barrie Capital Needs**: Advocating for new elementary and high school capacity to address rapid residential development in South Barrie.
* **Equity and Inclusion**: Championing student voice, diverse learning pathways, and human rights within the school board system.
* **Parent & Community Consultation**: Facilitating open forums with Parent Involvement Committees (PIC) and local school councils.
