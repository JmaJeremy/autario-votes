**Doug Rawson** is the **Mayor of the Town of Penetanguishene**, elected in October 2022. Prior to his election as Mayor, he served on town council representing Ward 2 and worked extensively in public healthcare administration.

### Municipal Leadership & Priorities
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Active member representing the historic bilingual community on County Council.
* **Francophone & Cultural Heritage**: Championing bilingual municipal services and preserving Penetanguishene's rich 400-year French and Métis history.
* **Waypoint Centre & Institutional Relations**: Collaborating with regional employers including Waypoint Centre for Mental Health Care and the Central North Correctional Centre.
