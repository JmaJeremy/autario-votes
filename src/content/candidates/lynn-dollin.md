**Lynn Dollin** is the **Mayor of the Town of Innisfil**, first elected as Mayor in 2018 and re-elected in 2022. Prior to her mayoral service, she served as Deputy Mayor and as a town councillor for over two decades.

### Municipal Leadership & Focus
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Serves on the 32-member County Council representing Innisfil.
* **Association of Municipalities of Ontario (AMO)**: Past President of AMO, championing municipal infrastructure funding and provincial-municipal fiscal partnerships.
* **Key Priorities**: Expanding community healthcare access, managing fast-paced lakefront growth along Lake Simcoe, transit expansion, and preservation of agricultural and shoreline assets.
