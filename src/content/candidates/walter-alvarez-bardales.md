**Walter Alvarez-Bardales** was the Ontario Liberal Party candidate for **Simcoe North** in the 2025 general election, winning 13,328 votes (27.56%).

A dedicated educator and community advocate with deep ties to North Simcoe, Alvarez-Bardales focused his campaign on strengthening rural emergency medical services, protecting Georgian Bay's freshwater shoreline, and expanding vocational and apprenticeship training for youths across Orillia, Midland, and Penetanguishene.
