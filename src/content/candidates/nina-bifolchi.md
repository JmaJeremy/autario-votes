**Nina Bifolchi** was a mayoral candidate in 2022, receiving 2,427 votes (30.78%). She previously served as Mayor of Wasaga Beach from 2018 to 2022 and as a town councillor from 2010 to 2014, overseeing construction of the twin-pad arena and public library.
