**Jennifer Coughlin** serves as the **Mayor of Springwater Township** and **Deputy Warden of the County of Simcoe** for the 2023–2024 and 2025–2026 terms.

Coughlin works closely with Warden Basil Clarke and the 32-member County Council on regional governance, environmental initiatives, and long-term infrastructure planning. In Springwater, her focus includes managing residential growth in communities like Midhurst and Elmvale, upgrading local road networks, and supporting community sports and recreation facilities.
