**Ted Walker** is the **Mayor of the Township of Tay**, first elected Mayor in 2018 and re-elected in 2022. He brings more than four decades of municipal administration experience, having served as Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) and clerk for various municipalities in Simcoe County prior to entering elected office.

### Governance & Community Focus
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Active member representing the communities of Port McNicoll, Victoria Harbour, Waubaushene, and Waverley.
* **Fiscal Responsibility & Transparency**: Advocating for stable property tax rates, modernization of municipal assets, and shoreline safety along Georgian Bay.
* **Heritage & Trail Preservation**: Championing the Tay Shore Trail and historical preservation around historic SS Keewatin and Huronia heritage landmarks.
