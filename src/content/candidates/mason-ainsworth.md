**Mason Ainsworth** was a candidate for Mayor of the City of Orillia in 2022, earning 2,878 votes (33.95%).

Ainsworth previously served two consecutive terms on Orillia City Council as a Ward 3 Councillor from 2014 to 2022, known for active constituent engagement, community events, and downtown revitalization.
