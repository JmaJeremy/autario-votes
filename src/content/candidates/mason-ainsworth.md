**Mason Ainsworth** is a certified candidate for Councillor in Ward 2 of the City of Orillia in the 2026 municipal election.

Ainsworth previously served two consecutive terms on Orillia City Council representing Ward 3 from 2014 to 2022. During his council tenure, Ainsworth was known for hands-on constituent advocacy, youth and community sports engagement, and active support for local events and neighbourhood revitalisation.

In the 2026 municipal election, Ainsworth has filed nomination papers to return to municipal service as Councillor representing the electors of Ward 2.
