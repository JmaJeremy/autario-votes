**Jodi Lloyd** serves as a Trustee on the Simcoe County District School Board (SCDSB) and represents the English public school electorate across the **City of Orillia, Township of Ramara, and Township of Severn**. The Board's current Chair is Brandy Rafeek.

### Board Leadership & Governance
* **Trustee**: Has served multiple terms on the Board of Trustees, participating in education policy, student achievement, and capital construction decisions for over 50,000 students across Simcoe County.
* **School Capital Funding**: Actively advocates with the Ontario Ministry of Education for new school builds, modular additions, and pupil accommodation planning in growing communities.
* **Special Education & Mental Health**: Prioritizes mental health support teams, special education funding allocations, and safe learning environments.
