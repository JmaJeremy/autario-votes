**Jodi Lloyd** serves as the **Chair of the Simcoe County District School Board (SCDSB)** and represents the English public school electorate across the **City of Orillia, Township of Ramara, and Township of Severn**.

### Board Leadership & Governance
* **Chairperson**: Has served multiple terms as Chair of the Board of Trustees, overseeing education policy, student achievement, and capital construction for over 50,000 students across Simcoe County.
* **School Capital Funding**: Actively advocates with the Ontario Ministry of Education for new school builds, modular additions, and pupil accommodation planning in growing communities.
* **Special Education & Mental Health**: Prioritizes mental health support teams, special education funding allocations, and safe learning environments.
