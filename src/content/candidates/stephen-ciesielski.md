**Stephen Ciesielski** represented the Green Party of Ontario in **Barrie—Innisfil** in the February 2025 general election, earning 1,655 votes.

Ciesielski focused on Lake Simcoe watershed protection, stopping environmentally damaging sprawl, investing in active transit (cycling and walking infrastructure) in South Barrie and Innisfil, and promoting renewable energy solutions.
