**Sandie Macdonald** is the **Mayor of the Township of Essa**, having served as Mayor since 2018 and winning re-election in 2022. She previously served as Deputy Mayor and as a ward councillor for Essa Township.

### Key Roles & Focus
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Represents Angus, Baxter, Colwell, Utopia, and surrounding agricultural areas on County Council.
* **CFB Borden Partnership**: Works closely with Canadian Forces Base Borden, one of Canada's largest military training institutions located within Essa Township boundaries.
* **Smart Growth & Infrastructure**: Managing population expansion in Angus while maintaining Essa's strong agricultural roots and rural heritage.
