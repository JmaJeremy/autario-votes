**Brian Smith** is the **Mayor of the Town of Wasaga Beach**, re-elected to the mayoral position in October 2022 after previously serving as Mayor from 2014 to 2018.

### Key Initiatives
* **Beachfront Revitalization**: Leading commercial and residential redevelopment initiatives around the world-famous Main Beach area (Beach Area 1 and 2).
* **Wasaga Stars Arena & Library**: Overseeing the completion and operation of Wasaga Beach's landmark twin-pad arena and public library community hub.
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Serving on County Council and advocating for infrastructure funding to support year-round tourism and expanding permanent resident demographics.
