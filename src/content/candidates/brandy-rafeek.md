**Brandy Rafeek** serves as the **Vice-Chair of the Simcoe County District School Board (SCDSB)** and is the elected trustee representing **Adjala-Tosorontio, Clearview, CFB Borden, and Essa Township**.

### Education Advocacy & Priorities
* **Board Vice-Chair**: Re-elected by her fellow trustees to serve in executive leadership for the SCDSB board.
* **Military Families Support**: Strong advocate for military children and families attending schools connected to Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Borden.
* **Rural School Programming**: Championing equal access to technology, specialized courses, and school transportation for rural students across south-western Simcoe County.
