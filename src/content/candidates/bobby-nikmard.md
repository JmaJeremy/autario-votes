**Bobby (Babak) Nikmard** represented the Ontario New Democratic Party (NDP) in the September 3, 2026 provincial by-election in **York—Simcoe**, receiving 974 votes.

Nikmard campaigned on fighting privatization in public health services, expanding affordable transit options connecting Bradford to York Region, and protecting prime agricultural soils in the Holland Marsh.
