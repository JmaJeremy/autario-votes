**Dane Lee** ran as the Ontario Liberal Party candidate for **Barrie—Innisfil** in the February 2025 provincial general election, earning 10,613 votes (26.05%).

Lee's campaign emphasized public healthcare preservation, affordability for families, support for small businesses in South Barrie and Alcona, and urgent environmental protection for Lake Simcoe.
