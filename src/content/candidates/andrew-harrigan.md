**Andrew Harrigan** was the Ontario NDP candidate for **Barrie—Innisfil** in the February 2025 general election, securing 5,442 votes (13.36%).

Harrigan is a grassroots community advocate focused on tenant protections, affordable housing solutions in South Barrie and Innisfil, living wages, and protecting the Lake Simcoe watershed from disruptive development.
