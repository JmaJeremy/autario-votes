**Gerry Marshall** contested the Barrie mayoral race in 2022, securing 2,745 votes (8.74%).

Marshall has deep Simcoe County leadership experience, previously serving as Mayor of Penetanguishene (2010–2018) and Warden of Simcoe County (2014–2018). He has focused on regional transit integration, manufacturing revitalization, and fiscal accountability.
