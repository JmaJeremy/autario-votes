**Ted Crysler** was the Ontario Liberal Party candidate for **Simcoe—Grey** in the February 27, 2025 provincial general election, finishing second with 18,507 votes (32.44%).

Crysler is a municipal lawyer, community volunteer, and former municipal candidate. His campaign focused on healthcare access, funding for regional hospitals in Collingwood and Alliston, protection of prime agricultural land in Clearview and Essa, and investment in public school infrastructure.
