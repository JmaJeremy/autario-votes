**Caroline Mulroney** served as the Member of Provincial Parliament for **York—Simcoe** from 2018 until her resignation from provincial politics on June 5, 2026.

During her eight years in the Legislative Assembly of Ontario, Mulroney held prominent senior cabinet positions in Premier Doug Ford's government:
* **President of the Treasury Board** (2023–2026)
* **Minister of Francophone Affairs** (2018–2026)
* **Minister of Transportation** (2019–2023)
* **Attorney General of Ontario** (2018–2019)

As Minister of Transportation and MPP for York—Simcoe, Mulroney was the leading champion for the planning and procurement of the **Bradford Bypass**, connecting Highway 400 and Highway 404 through Simcoe County. She was re-elected in February 2025 before stepping down in June 2026, precipitating the September 2026 by-election won by Susan Lahey.
