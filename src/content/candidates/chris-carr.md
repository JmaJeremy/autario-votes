**Chris Carr** ran as the Green Party of Ontario candidate for **Simcoe North** in the February 2025 provincial election, securing 3,214 votes (6.65%).

Carr's campaign focused on protecting Lake Couchiching and Georgian Bay water basins, halting aggregate extraction in ecologically sensitive areas, promoting regenerative farming, and investing in energy efficiency retrofits for North Simcoe homes.
