**Barry Ward** was a candidate for Mayor of the City of Barrie in the 2022 municipal election, finishing a close second with 12,624 votes (40.19%).

Prior to his mayoral bid, Ward served as Barrie's Deputy Mayor (2018–2022) and represented Ward 4 on Barrie City Council for 22 years from 2000 to 2022. He is a veteran local journalist and respected civic leader.
