**Benten Tinkler** ran as the Ontario New Democratic Party candidate for **Simcoe—Grey** in the February 2025 provincial election, securing 3,264 votes.

Tinkler campaigned on defending workers' rights, capping rent increases, expanding publicly funded mental healthcare, and preserving natural heritage areas and prime farmland against suburban sprawl across Simcoe County.
