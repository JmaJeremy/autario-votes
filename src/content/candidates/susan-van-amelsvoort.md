**Susan van Amelsvoort** is an elected Trustee for the **Simcoe Muskoka Catholic District School Board (SMCDSB)**, representing separate school supporters across **Barrie Wards 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5**.

### Catholic Education Advocacy
* **Faith-Based Education**: Championing Catholic identity, values-based curriculum, and community outreach through regional parish partnerships.
* **School Facility Enhancements**: Supporting capital investment and modernization for elementary and secondary Catholic schools across north and central Barrie.
* **Student Well-Being**: Advocating for inclusive learning environments, mental health initiatives, and athletic/arts program funding.
