**Bill Gordon** is the **Mayor of the Town of Midland**, elected to lead the town in October 2022 after serving as a town councillor from 2018 to 2022. He is a former police officer with extensive public sector experience.

### Priorities & Governance
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Represents North Simcoe's largest urban centre on County Council.
* **Harbourfront Redevelopment**: Spearheading the long-term vision for Midland's picturesque Georgian Bay waterfront and historical downtown core.
* **Community Safety & Housing**: Focusing on downtown revitalization, addressing homelessness and addictions services in partnership with county paramedics and social services.
