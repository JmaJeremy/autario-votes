**Jordi Malcolm** was the Ontario NDP candidate for **Simcoe North** in the February 2025 general election, obtaining 4,813 votes (9.95%).

Malcolm's campaign emphasized public healthcare preservation, expanding rural transit links across North Simcoe communities, affordable housing initiatives in Orillia and Midland, and Indigenous reconciliation efforts with First Nations across the Georgian Bay area.
