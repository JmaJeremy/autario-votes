**Tim Grant** ran as the Green Party of Ontario candidate for **Barrie—Springwater—Oro-Medonte** in the February 2025 general election, receiving 1,637 votes.

Grant's platform advocated for protecting the Oro Moraine and prime agricultural lands from highway and sprawl encroachment, preserving rural drinking water aquifers, and incentivizing local community solar installations.
