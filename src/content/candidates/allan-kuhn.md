**Allan Kuhn** was the Green Party of Ontario candidate for **Simcoe—Grey** in the February 2025 general election, receiving 3,154 votes.

Kuhn's platform prioritized climate action, ecological conservation across the Niagara Escarpment, defending wetlands and groundwater from overdevelopment, and expanding local green energy cooperatives.
