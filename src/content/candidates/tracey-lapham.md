**Tracey Lapham** ran as the Ontario NDP candidate for **Barrie—Springwater—Oro-Medonte** in the February 2025 provincial election, receiving 2,710 votes.

Lapham focused her platform on rural healthcare staffing shortages, addressing elder care in long-term care homes, public education funding, and expanding reliable rural broadband and clean energy infrastructure across Springwater and Oro-Medonte.
