**Matt Pearce** was the candidate for the Green Party of Ontario in the **York—Simcoe** provincial by-election on September 3, 2026, receiving 642 votes.

Pearce's campaign focused on defending the sensitive Lake Simcoe ecosystem, opposing environmentally costly highway projects across delicate wetlands, supporting green agricultural practices across the Holland Marsh, and funding public health and education.
