**Scott Anderson** is the **Mayor of the Township of Adjala-Tosorontio**, elected in October 2022 to represent the rural municipality in south-west Simcoe County.

### Leadership & Representation
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Represents Everett, Lisle, Rosemont, Glencairn, and surrounding rural communities on County Council.
* **Rural Services & Infrastructure**: Focused on bridge and road rehabilitation, broadband internet expansion, and sustainable rural taxation.
* **Conservation & Water Management**: Partnering with the Nottawasaga Valley Conservation Authority (NVCA) on river health and flood mitigation.
