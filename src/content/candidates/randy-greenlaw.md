**Randy Greenlaw** is the **Mayor of the Township of Oro-Medonte**, elected to office in October 2022. Prior to his election as Mayor, he served as a township councillor representing Ward 5.

### Key Focus Areas
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Member of the 32-seat County Council, advocating for rural road investments and environmental stewardship.
* **Short-Term Accommodation By-Laws**: Leading municipal initiatives to balance tourism with residential quiet enjoyment across Lake Simcoe and rural shoreline communities.
* **Environmental Protection**: Preserving the environmentally sensitive Oro Moraine, Lake Simcoe watershed, and prime agricultural soils.
