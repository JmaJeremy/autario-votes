**Richard Norcross** is the **Mayor of the Town of New Tecumseth**, serving as Mayor since 2018 and re-elected in October 2022. He previously served as Deputy Mayor and councillor representing Ward 6 (Beeton/rural).

### Key Roles & Responsibilities
* **County of Simcoe Council**: Long-time member of County Council representing South Simcoe communities (Alliston, Beeton, Tottenham).
* **Industrial & Automotive Hub**: Working closely with Honda of Canada Mfg. regarding massive electric vehicle (EV) supply chain investments and local industrial expansion.
* **Water & Wastewater Infrastructure**: Advancing major regional pipeline and water pipeline projects to secure reliable long-term servicing capacity for New Tecumseth's growing population.
